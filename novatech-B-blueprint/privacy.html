Privacy Policy & Data Governance

EFFECTIVE DATE: 1 SEPTEMBER 2026 // AUDITED STANDARD: SOC 2 TYPE II COMPLIANT
NOTICE: This policy articulates our operational data practices across client engagements, infrastructure telemetry, and web interactions. NovaTech Solutions executes client-specific Data Processing Addendums (DPAs) upon engagement.

§1. Information We Collect

We collect technical discovery information directly when you submit our contact or careers forms: work email, organization name, technical requirements, and communication logs. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers or sell client data under any circumstances.

§2. Client Infrastructure Telemetry

During active engineering engagements, all telemetry collected through OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, or Grafana is routed to customer-controlled VPC boundaries. NovaTech personnel access staging and production environments exclusively via hardware security tokens (FIDO2/WebAuthn) and ephemeral zero-trust certificates.

§3. Data Retention & Disposal

Engagement logs, diagnostic captures, and synthetic test datasets are retained solely for the contractual engagement period plus 90 days for audit verification, after which they are cryptographically shredded in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 standards.

§4. Texas Legal Governance

NovaTech Solutions Inc. is registered in the State of Texas. Data governance practices comply with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) and applicable federal statutes. Inquiries may be addressed to privacy@novatech-solutions.io.
