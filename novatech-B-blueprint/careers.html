01 // Engineering Culture & Roles

Engineering with people who take pride in operational craft.

We operate as a compact, senior-only engineering practice. Zero bureaucratic ticket churning, no arbitrary deadlines, and a culture built around verifiable facts, clean code, and fair compensation.

BEHAVIOR 01 // PEER REVIEWS

Thorough, Respectful Code Reviews

Pull requests are reviewed within four hours. We evaluate for architectural correctness, race conditions, and test coverage—never for subjective aesthetic preferences.

BEHAVIOR 02 // ON-CALL FAIRNESS

1.5x Shift Premium & Recovery Time

Our on-call rotation is shared exclusively among senior architects who built the systems. Shifts are compensated at 1.5x standard rates with mandatory sleep recovery hours.

BEHAVIOR 03 // SUSTAINABLE PACE

Strict 40-Hour Engineering Week

We decline unrealistic client schedules. We do not work weekends or expect late-night replies to non-emergency messages. We plan capacity with realistic engineering estimates.

BEHAVIOR 04 // LEARNING DIRECTIVE

$2,000 Annual Learning Budget

Every engineer receives an annual budget of $2,000 for technical literature, specialized conferences, or home lab hardware without approval hurdles.
